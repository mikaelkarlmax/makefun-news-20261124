# THE WORLD IN ONE DAY — Tuesday 24 November 2026

A single 1024×1024 broadsheet poster of **46 stories from one day**, every one verified
against reporting dated **Tuesday 24 November 2026** — and nothing from any other day.

## What the poster shows

- **Masthead + giant date line** — "THE WORLD IN ONE DAY", 46 stories, 7 regions.
- **Red subhead** with the three leads of the day: the Houthis hitting Riyadh's tallest
  tower again, Nvidia giving back 5.9% after its historic rebound, and CAS restoring
  Senegal as African champions.
- **Six big numbers**: 46 stories · −5.9% Nvidia · 191,780 China hybrid cap · >720,000
  modelled Russian war dead (leaked registers) · 151 Donald–Salt T20 stand · 60%
  Albanese disapproval.
- **The day in UTC** — overnight, morning, afternoon, evening, 21:00Z Wall Street close,
  21:13 GMT Africa update, 23:59 wires close. Only verified times, no invented ones.
- **Region strip** — Europe 10 · Middle East 6 · Asia-Pacific 8 · Americas 10 · Africa 5 ·
  Oceania 5 · World 2.
- **3 lead cards + 12 illustrated cards** — all art drawn in code, no photographs, no
  copyrighted assets.
- **31 more stories**, numbered 1–46 with no gaps and no repeats.
- **Dot-matrix world map** with numbered pins for the 15 illustrated stories.
- **DATE CHECK** — the stories we left out because they are *not* from Tuesday 24
  November (Nvidia's +31.5% reinstatement on Monday, the COP31 collapse on Sunday,
  the Merkury sinking on Sunday, and 15 more), plus a certainty legend
  (REPORTED / CLAIM / FORECAST / LEAK).

## Files

- `poster.html` — the poster source (static HTML + inline SVG, no network).
- `stories.json` — all 46 stories with headlines, details, certainty labels and sources.
- `entry.png` — the rendered poster, 1024×1024, the image the program copies.
- `main.ts` — copies `entry.png` to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png`, deterministically.

## Verification

Every story was checked against reporting dated 24 November 2026. Stories first
reported on other days were swapped for Tuesday-dated replacements (Italy–Libya,
European gas prices, Trump's Nvidia stock, MSCI–Indonesia, Marcos's veto threat,
Anwar on the South China Sea, Brazil's real, the Huang backlash, Walmart's
Thanksgiving rush, Congo's cobalt ban, the Madlanga Commission). Figures were
corrected where the wires disagreed with early drafts: Nvidia −5.9% (close),
S&P −0.5% (close), Kospi +6.3%, leak modelled above 720,000, hybrid cap 191,780,
Albanese net −28, T20 stand 151.
